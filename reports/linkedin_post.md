# LinkedIn draft — Building Faster Fund

**Ontario set housing targets for 50 municipalities. Its October 2026 update confirmed Building Faster Fund allocations for 18.**

The update covered **2025 housing performance**.

The Building Faster Fund (BFF) was a three-year Ontario program of up to **C$1.2 billion** for housing-enabling and community infrastructure.

Municipalities needed to reach **at least 80% of their annual housing target** to qualify for funding. They did not need to reach 100%.

Kitchener reported **2,516 construction starts and 489 additional residential units**: 3,005 units against a target of 3,500. That put it at **85.9%**, with **C$8.01 million** in funding announced.

The official measure also counts institutional categories, including long-term-care beds. Additional residential units are separate from those beds and can provide homes for families.

I mapped the 18 municipalities named in the October 5 update. Blue marks confirmed allocations; grey is geographic context.

Further reviews were pending, so this is not a count of how many municipalities failed. An announced allocation also does not establish that the money has been received.

Code, data, maps and sources are in the first comment.
